Help: method

  1. Pick the stage. Its targets come from the breed guide.
  2. Fix the small items first: salt, DCP, grit, amino acids, binder and enzyme.
  3. Balance protein between cereals (maize, broken rice) and protein sources (soya, LC40).
  4. Add DCP until available P is on target, then grit for the rest of the Ca.
  5. Top up lysine and methionine with synthetics instead of adding more soya.
  6. Read the status chips, adjust, and repeat until every row says OK.
Edit ingredient nutrient values

Values are per 100 kg (%), except ME in kcal/kg. Defaults follow the Skylark ingredient table you shared; LC40 uses your lab test (its lysine and methionine are estimates). Replace any value with your own lab results.

Raw material master

Nutrients are % of the material (ME in kcal/kg). Max % is the most allowed in the total feed for each stage group; 0 = not allowed. Changes apply to every formula at once. Built-in names are fixed; your own materials can be renamed or deleted.
